A. Read-only table + edit drawer (recommended)

The table is for reading: formatted ₱, right-aligned, empty steps shown as a quiet "—". Hovering a row shows Edit, which opens a side panel for one grade with all 8 steps and the impact count shown before you save. This is the usual pattern for payroll and compensation tables (Workday, BambooHR, Rippling), and it makes a mistyped digit hard to save by accident.

B. Grade list + detail panel

A list of all 33 grades on the left with no pagination, and the selected grade's steps and usage on the right. It's calm and gives each grade room to show context, like how many job posts it feeds. The cost is that you lose the side-by-side schedule view, so you can't compare grades across steps at a glance.

C. Spreadsheet edit + save bar

Click any cell to edit it in place, with changed cells highlighted. A sticky bar collects every change and asks for one review-and-confirm, instead of a dialog per cell. It's the fastest way to enter a whole new CSC tranche (Airtable and Google Sheets work this way), but it's easier to change something by mistake.
