Applicant tracker: how should all applications under one Reference No. appear?

Juan searched ABYAN-731-052. He has 3 applications. Today the page shows only the newest one, followed by its timeline, documents, schedule and notice sections.

A

Summary list on top, pick one to see its details

Every application is visible at a glance, and you open one at a time. The one that needs attention (Pending or scheduled) opens by default, otherwise the newest. The existing detail sections are reused as they are.

Compact, but you can't see the other applications' statuses without clicking each tab. Long position names get cramped on a phone.

Nothing is hidden, but the page gets very long. With 3 or more applications, a resubmission request is easy to scroll past.
